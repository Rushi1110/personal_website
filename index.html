    PRD · Ananya v1

    Shipped Owner: Rushabh Parikh · Jumbo Homes · 2025

    Ananya: an AI agent that books the site visit

    Problem

    Home buyers message many listings, then go quiet. Relationship managers can't reply to every lead fast enough, so most leads never become a site visit, and the site visit is where deals happen.

    Success metric

    Lead → site-visit rate. Reply quality and speed matter only as far as they move it.

    What Ananya can do

    • search_listings across 800+ active homes
    • commute and schools checks for each shortlist
    • book_visit with a relationship manager
    • handoff to a human when a question needs judgement

    Result

    4.5%→10.3%

    lead → visit, first month after launch
