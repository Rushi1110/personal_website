    Lab notebook · Chakr Innovation · 2024–25

    Technical Project Manager, Moonshot R&D

    Ran a moonshot lab on aluminium and air

    Aluminium-air batteries could make EVs cheaper, if the anode performs and the aluminium is affordable. I led the six-person team working on both, and worked closely with the CEO.

    −1.35 V
    −1.35 V · where we started−1.70 V · where we got

    Illustration, not lab data. A more negative potential meant fewer parasitic reactions and a more efficient cell.

    • 83% of pure-Al performance… from scrap aluminium. The unlock for commercial viability.
    • −40% input cost after re-engineering sourcing in China
    • ML model bridging test conditions: 5× faster, 90% cheaper testing
    • Month-long two-wheeler pilot → EOIs from Amazon & Swiggy
