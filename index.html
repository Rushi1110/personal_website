Case study · Jumbo Homes · 2025

Ananya books the visit.

Ananya is Jumbo's agentic home-buying assistant. It learns what a buyer needs, checks listings, commutes and schools, and books a site visit. In real estate, the site visit is the step that decides whether a deal happens, so that is what we built it to do.

ananya / session Example
  1. Buyer

    Looking for a 3BHK near Whitefield, under ₹1.2 Cr. My wife works at ITPL and good schools matter.

  2. search_listings(zone="Whitefield", bhk=3, budget≤1.2Cr) 14 homes
  3. commute(to="ITPL", mode="car", peak=true) 5 under 20 min
  4. schools(radius_km=2, board=["CBSE","ICSE"]) 3 fit
  5. Ananya

    Three homes fit. The best match is 11 minutes from ITPL at peak hour, with two CBSE schools within 1.5 km, at ₹1.12 Cr. Want to see it this Saturday?

  6. Buyer

    Saturday at 11 works.

  7. book_visit(slot="Sat 11:00", assign="relationship_manager") Booked
  8. Ananya

    Done. Your relationship manager will meet you at the gate on Saturday at 11. I've sent the location and the other two options on WhatsApp.

Operating principles

How I build AI products.

  • Learned at Jumbo

    Choose the metric before the model.

    Ananya was judged on booked visits. Reply quality mattered only when it moved that number.

  • Learned at Chakr

    Write down what good looks like.

    In the lab, the test protocol decided what we believed. With LLMs, evals do that job. If the team can't define good, it can't ship it.

  • Learned at Jumbo

    Unit economics are part of the product.

    I designed sales incentives that pay above market and stay CM2 positive. An AI feature that loses money on every call isn't finished.

  • Learned at OLA

    Use models to buy back time.

    The crash model told us which designs deserved a full simulation. Most useful AI works that way: it points people's time at the right problem.
