    PRD · Ananya

    Shipped Owner: Rushabh Parikh · Jumbo Homes · 2025

    Ananya: the agent that books the visit

    Problem

    Buyers enquire, then vanish. Lead-to-visit sat at 4.5%, and the site visit is where deals happen.

    What was leaking

    1. Fixed visit slots. Buyers don't live in 30-minute windows. Free-flowing scheduling moved the number most.
    2. Irrelevant homes. We tuned which properties each buyer sees.
    3. Bad photos. Which is how Majnu Bhai was born.

    Scope

    Answer anything about a property, honestly, and schedule the visit. That's the whole job, on purpose.

    Evals

    An LLM judge scores every conversation on one question: did Ananya do what it was supposed to?

    Result

    4.5%→~10%

    lead → visit, holding week after week
