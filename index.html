    Lab notebook · Chakr Innovation · 2024–25

    Technical Project Manager, Moonshot R&D

    Tried to power EVs with aluminium and thin air

    An aluminium-air battery makes electricity from aluminium metal and oxygen in the air. It's cheap and packs a lot of energy, but the metal also corrodes, wasting itself as hydrogen gas. I led the six-person R&D team that cut the waste and the cost.

    −1.35 V
    −1.35 V · where we started−1.70 V · where we got

    Drag right: a more negative anode potential means less aluminium lost as H₂ and a more efficient cell. Illustration, not lab data.

    • 83%of peak performance from scrap aluminium, the key to commercial viability
    • −40%raw-material cost. I built the supply chain on the ground in China
    • 5× fasterand 90% cheaper testing, using an ML model I built
    • Amazon, Swiggyinterested after a month-long vehicle pilot. I ran the go-to-market myself
