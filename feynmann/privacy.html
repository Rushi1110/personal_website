Your work, and who sees it

The Feynmann pen-test pilot. Last updated 7 November 2026.

What we keep

  • Your Google name and email, to know it's you. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • Your pen strokes on each test: where and when you wrote, how hard you pressed, and what you erased. This is how Feynmann sees where the time went and where you hesitated.
  • Your answers, and how long each question took.
  • Your review with Feynmann: what you wrote and said, and what Feynmann replied, question by question.
  • What the AI models answered about your working, so we can check them and make them better.

Where it's kept

On Cloudflare, in an account run by Rushabh Parikh, who builds Feynmann. Not in any other company's database, not sold, not used for advertising.

Who sees it

  • You. Your papers and reviews, from any device you sign in on.
  • Teachers in the pilot see each student's papers, mistakes and reviews, so they can plan the next class. During the pilot there is one class: every teacher on the list sees every student on it.
  • Rushabh, to run the pilot and improve Feynmann.

The AI models

To read your working and talk it through, a picture of your page and the review go to AI models (OpenAI's GPT models) through OpenRouter. We send them only to providers that don't keep it or train on it. They see the work, not your email.

Videos are played from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode, and only when you open one.

Taking it back

Write to rushabhparikh10@gmail.com and we'll delete your papers, strokes and reviews, or send you a copy of them.
