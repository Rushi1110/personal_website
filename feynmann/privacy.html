Privacy

Feynmann, a learning app by Rushabh Parikh. Last updated 3 October 2026.

When you sign in with Google

We receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to know who is using Feynmann.

What we record

To learn what people want to study and improve Feynmann, we record, against your email:

  • topics you search for, and the part of a topic you choose to learn
  • the learning plan Feynmann writes for you (its parts and chapter titles)
  • your placement result, and the chapters you finish with their test scores
  • feedback you send us, and whether you join the waitlist

This is stored in a private Google Sheet that only the app's owner can open.

Free credits

Signed-in learners get free credits for AI use on our account. To keep it fair we count, against your email, how many you have used. That count is kept on Cloudflare, where Feynmann's server runs.

All shelves

When Feynmann writes a notebook, its plan, lessons, quizzes and suggested videos are added to All shelves so other learners can study them for free. They are shared without your name or email, and without anything you answered, wrote or said.

What we never record

  • what you write or say when you explain an idea back
  • the questions you ask the tutor
  • your OpenAI or YouTube API keys. They stay in your own browser; requests made with them pass through our server on the way to OpenAI or YouTube without being stored.

Where your notebooks live

Your notebooks and progress (plans, lessons, which chapters you've finished, test scores, what's due for review) are saved to your account on Feynmann's server, on Cloudflare, so they follow you to any device you sign in on. A copy is kept in your browser so the app is fast. What you write or say when explaining is not saved, and your API keys stay only in the browser you typed them into. Requests to OpenAI and YouTube go to those companies under their own privacy policies.

Removing your data

Email rushabhparikh10@gmail.com and we'll delete every row recorded for your email, and your saved notebooks. To clear what's in your browser, use Settings → Save a backup first if you want one, then clear this site's data in your browser.
